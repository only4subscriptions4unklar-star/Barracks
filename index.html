Since Dutugemunu Street

A neighbourhood house, kept to a standard

Barracks began with a single rack of dry-fit tees on Dutugemunu Street — and never left the neighbourhood. Two stores now stand a flyover apart, cutting trousers, shirting and essentials for the men of Colombo and beyond.

Every piece is chosen for the island it will live on: linen that breathes at noon, twill that holds a crease through a working day, cloth that survives the wash line.

Considered cloth

The Barracks Standard

I.

True linen, no shortcuts

100% linen — never a poly blend — woven open to breathe through 30° humidity and pressed to look sharp at an evening reception.

II.

Cloth with substance

220gsm combed cotton jersey and 320gsm heavyweight twill: fabric that hangs straight, holds its silhouette and never turns sheer.

III.

Finished to last

French seams, pre-shrunk and colour-locked for Sri Lankan wash cycles, ribbing that keeps its shape past fifty washes.

Particulars

The fine print

For reasons of hygiene: underwear, socks and body sprays are final sale. Pieces marked “final” may be exchanged for size but not refunded.

Card and Koko/Mintpay payments reverse to the original method within three to five working days of the piece reaching us. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer — send your account details on WhatsApp.

That is ours to mend. Send a photograph on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and the correct piece ships immediately, with collection of the other — no forms, no argument.

Bring the piece and its tags to Kohuwala or Dehiwala within seven days, any day 10:30–20:30. In-store exchanges are immediate.

Size exchanges remain free as usual. Should one trouser of a pair be returned for refund, the remaining trouser reverts to its single price and the difference is deducted.
